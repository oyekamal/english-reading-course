# Level 1 Mastery Check — Full Instrument

Administered as **L1.14** (see `lessons/L1.14-mastery-check-lesson.md` for the session
framing). This file is the actual test: word lists, dictation, scoring, and the reteach map.

**Ground rule for the tutor/parent/self-learner running this:** no hints, no "sound it out
again more slowly" prompts mid-item, no pointing to a similar word on the page. Score exactly
what the learner produces on the first try. This is diagnostic, not a teaching moment — teaching
happens in the reteach step afterward, not during scoring.

**Six components, scored and gated separately** (DESIGN.md rule 8 — never average decoding and
comprehension, or real-word and pseudoword accuracy, into one blended number):

1. Letter-sound check
2. Real-word reading
3. Pseudoword ("alien word") reading
4. Heart-word check
5. Dictation
6. b/d/p/q discrimination

---

## 1. Letter-sound check (31 items)

Show each in mixed, non-alphabetical order. Ask "what does it say?" — not "what is it called?"

a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q(u) r s t u v w x y z ck ff ll ss zz

**Score:** ___/31. **Threshold: ≥28/31 (90%).** (26 single letters + ck, ff, ll, ss, zz = 31;
`qu` is covered via the "q(u)" item rather than counted separately — README's exit criteria
list "all 26 letter-sounds... plus ck, ff, ll, ss, zz, qu," and this is now a complete,
independently-verified count against that list, not just ck/ff as an earlier draft of this
check tested.)

## 2. Real-word reading (20 words, one per lesson's new sound)

sat · pin · mad · dog · sock · cut · hen · fib · bell · hats · jog · van · wig · box · yak ·
zip · quiz · big · dip · mess

**Score:** ___/20. **Threshold: ≥18/20 (90%).**

## 3. Pseudoword ("alien word") reading (10 words)

taf · nid · gof · kec · rup · haz · vin · quob · fex · dov

**Score:** ___/10. **Threshold: ≥9/10 (90%).** This is the single most important number on the
whole check — real words can sometimes be recognized by shape/memory even without full
decoding, but a pseudoword score at 90%+ is the actual proof the skill is decoding, not
memorization (`research/01` §1, Share's Self-Teaching Hypothesis).

## 4. Heart-word check (24 words)

**Part A — isolated reading** (show each word alone, mixed order):
a · I · the · is · to · of · and · was · said · you · are · he · she · we · me · be · do ·
what · they · one · have · go · no · so

**Part B — in context** (read the sentence, learner reads back the bolded word only, to
confirm it isn't just a memorized list order): "**The** dog ran." / "**They** are at the dock."
/ "**What** did you get?" / "**Have** you got a map?" / "**Go** to the dock." / "I **said** so."

**Score:** ___/24 (Part A) + ___/6 (Part B). **Threshold: ≥22/24 and ≥5/6 (≈90% each part).**

## 5. Dictation (5 words + 1 sentence)

**Words** (say each once, learner writes it): hat · bug · fox · jazz · sock
**Sentence** (say the whole sentence once at natural speed, then once more slowly if genuinely
needed — score whichever attempt is used): **"The dog and the pig sat in the mud."**

**Scoring:** 1 point per correctly spelled word (5 possible) + up to 5 points for the sentence
(1 point per correctly spelled word within it, capitalization/period not required for Level 1).
**Score:** ___/10. **Threshold: ≥9/10 (90%).**

## 6. b/d/p/q discrimination (8 items)

**Reading (4):** show "bat," "dip," "pat," "quiz" in mixed order with several other letters
nearby on the page as visual distractors — learner names each correctly.
**Writing (4):** dictate "bag," "dog," "pin," "quit" — learner writes each with the correct
letter shapes.

**Score:** ___/8. **Threshold: ≥7/8 (≈90%).**

## 7. Connected-text read (not scored numerically — observational only)

Have the learner read one full passage aloud, their choice of track. This is a confidence/
fluency-feel check, not a scored gate — note anything striking (very slow, very smooth, lots of
self-correction) for the tutor's own notes, but do not let it override the six scored
components above.

**Track A (child):**
> Sam has a dog. The dog sat. Sam got a cat. The cat and the dog run and run. Is the cat mad
> at the dog? No, the cat is not mad. They sit in the sun. Sam said, "Go, dog! Go, cat!"

**Track B (adult):**
> Dan has a job at the dock. Nat has a job at the dock. Dan gets a box. Nat gets a van. Dan has
> the box. Nat has the van. Dan and Nat fix the van. It was a big job.

> **Decodability note:** "too," "for," and "done" were deliberately avoided in both passages
> above — each contains a spelling-sound pattern (long oo, r-controlled "or," silent-e
> irregular) that isn't taught until Level 3 or Level 4. A connected-text check at the end of
> Level 1 must stay just as strictly decodable as every other lesson's practice text
> (DESIGN.md rule 2) — this is the mastery check, not an exception to it.

**All seven rows "Y" → placed into Level 2** (course/level-2/, "Sounds Together").

**Any row "N" → do not advance.** Use the reteach map below for that specific row only — do
not restart the whole level, and do not advance on the strength of other rows being strong (a
90%+ pseudoword score does not excuse a b/d/p/q failure, for example; each is measuring a
different thing).

## Reteach map

| Failed component | What it usually means | Reteach target |
|---|---|---|
| Letter-sounds, specific letters missed | Individual GPC(s) not yet automatic | Re-run that letter's original lesson's §2–4 (Hear it/Meet it/Blend it) only — not the whole lesson |
| Real words, misses cluster on one lesson's sound | Same as above, at the word level | That lesson's §4 Blend it, with 2x the review reps before re-checking |
| Real words, misses spread evenly across many lessons | Blending speed/fluency issue, not a specific-letter issue | General blending-speed practice: re-read several lessons' word lists at a slightly slower, more deliberate pace for a session, then re-check |
| Pseudowords low but real words fine | Likely whole-word memorization masking a real decoding gap — take this seriously even if real-word score looks fine | Full re-run of L1.02–L1.06's blending practice (the words most likely to have been memorized by shape) before re-checking pseudowords specifically |
| Heart words, isolated reading weak | Heart words not yet automatic | Re-run the specific lesson(s) that introduced the missed word(s), §6 only, with extra spaced repetition (see a word again 2–3 lessons later, not just once) |
| Heart words, context reading weak but isolated fine | Word recognized alone but not inside a sentence — a fluency/attention issue, not a knowledge gap | Re-read L1.13's or L1.14's connected-text passages (§7) a few more times, pointing at each heart word as it comes up |
| Dictation weak, reading fine | Spelling/encoding lags behind decoding — normal and expected to lag somewhat, per Ehri's phase theory, but not by this much | Extra §5 (Spell it) practice from 2–3 recent lessons before re-checking dictation only |
| b/d/p/q weak | The mirror-confusion genuinely hasn't resolved yet | Repeat L1.13 §3's anchor tricks for one more full session, with writing practice (not just reading) emphasized, before re-checking this component only |

**If 3+ components fail simultaneously:** step back further than a single-lesson reteach —
repeat L1.10–L1.13 as a block (the density of new rules/heart words in that stretch is the
most likely single point where things started slipping), then re-run the full mastery check
from scratch.

**Pause-safe reminder:** none of the above is a race. A learner who needs three passes at this
check before every row is "Y" has lost nothing — DESIGN.md rule 10 applies here as much as
anywhere else in the course.
