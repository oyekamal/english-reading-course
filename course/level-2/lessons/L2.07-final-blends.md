# L2.07 — Final blends (-st -nd -nt -mp -sk -lt -ft -lk)

**Goal:** Read and spell words that end in a two-consonant blend.
**New today:** final blends `-st -nd -nt -mp -sk -lt -ft -lk`
**Review:** initial blends; `sh, ch, tch, th, wh, ng, nk`; Level 1 letters
**Heart words:** pull, full
**Time:** 20–25 min
**Materials:** paper, pencil

## 1. Warm-up review (2–3 min)
Flash: initial blends `stop, plan, drum, skin, frog`. Read 5 review words: `black,
clap, swim, snap, trip`.

## 2. Hear it (2 min)
Say: "Listen: **nest**. Two sounds at the end this time — /s/ then /t/, both heard."
Same "two sounds, no gap" logic as initial blends (L2.06), just now at the end of
the word.

## 3. Meet it (3 min)
Show each final blend family in turn: `st, nd, nt, mp, sk, lt, ft, lk`. Say: "Same
rule as last lesson — two letters, two sounds, no vowel snuck in between them, this
time at the end of the word." Model segmenting a word into onset + vowel + final
blend (`n-e-st`) before blending it back together.

## 4. Blend it (5 min)
Real words: `nest, hand, sand, tent, hunt, jump, camp, desk, mask, belt, melt, gift,
soft, milk, silk`
Pseudowords: `fand, jelt, nusk, thomp, vilk`

## 5. Spell it (4 min)
Dictate: 1) sounds — `/st/`, `/nd/`, `/ft/` at the end of a word 2) words: `nest`,
`desk`, `gift` 3) sentence: **"I got a soft gift in the tent."**

## 6. Heart word(s) (2 min)
- **pull** — heart part: same `u` sound as "put/push" from L2.06 (short-oo); point
  out the pattern repeats a third time; `p` and `ll` are fully regular.
- **full** — same `u` heart pattern as "pull"; `f` and `ll` are fully regular.

## 7. Read it (5–8 min) — Fluency routine (model→echo→choral→independent, as L2.06)

**Track A (child)**
> Dad and Min set up a tent on the sand. / Min helps Dad pull the tent up. /
> "Pull fast!" / Dad says. / Min pulls and pulls until the tent is up. / Dad packs
> the milk and a gift in a bag. / Min helps hunt for the best spot to camp. / At
> dusk, / Min and Dad sit and rest, / full and glad.

*(60 words.)*

Questions: (1) What does Min help Dad pull up? (2) What does Dad pack in the bag?
(3) Why do you think Min and Dad feel "full and glad" at the end?

**Track B (adult)**
> Sam must pull the stock bin to the desk. / "Pull it fast," / his boss says. /
> Sam pulls and pulls until the bin is at the desk. / Sam checks the list, / packs
> the gift, / and gets it done. / The shop is full of stock, / and Sam is glad the
> job is done.

*(56 words.)*

Questions: (1) What does Sam pull to the desk? (2) What does Sam pack after the
bin is at the desk? (3) Why do you think Sam's boss wants it done fast?

## 8. Listen & Talk (5 min)
Read aloud only:

> When the school bake sale raised more money than anyone expected, the head
> teacher decided to give every extra rupee to the small clinic down the road. She
> was **grateful** — truly grateful — for how hard the students had worked, baking
> and selling from early morning until the last plate was empty. One student asked
> why she looked close to tears. "I am just so grateful," she said. "You worked all
> day, and now families who could not afford medicine will get help." The students
> felt proud too, and decided that being the reason someone else got help was
> better than any prize. They planned an even bigger, more grateful bake sale for
> next year.

**Tier-2 word: grateful** — friendly definition: "feeling thankful, glad someone did
something kind or helpful for you." Examples: "She was grateful for her friend's
help with the heavy bags." "He wrote a note to say how grateful he was." Your turn:
ask "What is something you feel grateful for today?"

Discussion questions: (1) Why was the head teacher grateful? (2) What did the
students decide to do the following year?

## 9. Check (2 min)
5 real words: `nest, hand, jump, desk, gift`
5 pseudowords: `fand, jelt, nusk, thomp, vilk`
1 dictated word: `milk`
**≥90%?** → L2.08. **<90%?** → Reteach: isolate the specific final-blend family that
failed and re-drill 5 fresh words in that family only before repeating steps 4–9.

## Tutor notes
- **Common error:** dropping the final consonant of the blend (reading "nes" for
  "nest"). Correction: point to both letters at the end and require both sounds
  before accepting the word as read.
- **Common error:** confusing an initial blend (L2.06) with a final blend at the
  same position in the word (rare, but check if a learner is reading blends only by
  shape, not position). Correction: re-point to where in the word each blend sits.
- **For adults:** "desk," "gift," and "shift" all becoming fully decodable this
  lesson is a strong motivational moment for a workplace-context adult learner —
  name it out loud.
