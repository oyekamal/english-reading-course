# L2.13 — Review (everything from Level 2)

**Goal:** Consolidate every grapheme and ending taught in Level 2 before the
mastery check — no new phonics content today, just cumulative retrieval practice at
the hardest level the learner has reached.
**New today:** nothing new — pure review
**Review:** `sh, ch, tch, th, wh, ng, nk`; initial and final 2-letter blends;
3-letter blends; `-s/-es, -ed, -ing/-er`; compounds and closed 2-syllable words
**Heart words:** again, friend, because (the last 3 of Level 2's 29)
**Time:** 25–30 min
**Materials:** paper, pencil

## 1. Warm-up review (2–3 min)
Flash one word from each Level 2 family, learner names the pattern before reading:
`shop (sh), catch (tch), think (th), when (wh), sing (nk/ng — pick one), strap
(3-blend), desk (final blend), wishes (-es), jumped (-ed), helper (-er), rabbit
(2-syllable)`.

## 2. Hear it (2 min)
Instead of one new sound, do a rapid-fire sound-spotting round: say 8 words from
across the level (`ship, chin, moth, whip, king, stop, gift, strap`) and have the
learner name which family each belongs to, purely by ear, before seeing the
spelling.

## 3. Meet it (3 min)
Put up a one-page grapheme chart covering every Level 2 pattern (`sh ch tch th wh ng
nk` + all blends + `-s/-es -ed -ing/-er` + compounds). Walk down it once, learner
reads each label aloud. This is a map of everything they now know, not new
teaching.

## 4. Blend it (5 min) — cumulative, spans every Level 2 pattern
Real words (one or more from every family taught this level): `wish, catch, thin,
when, king, sink, strap, string, desk, gift, wishes, boxes, jumped, wanted, helper,
jumping, rabbit, sunset`
Pseudowords (spanning multiple patterns each): `strishing, flonked, chuffer,
vandest, thramping`

## 5. Spell it (4 min)
Dictate: 1) sounds — one from each major family (`sh`, a blend, `-ed`) 2) words:
`catches`, `helped`, `napkin` 3) sentence: **"His friend wanted to help because I
was fixing the tent."**

## 6. Heart word(s) (2 min)
- **again** — heart part: contains the vowel team `ai` (a Level 3 topic, not yet
  taught) and doesn't say the sound the learner would expect from it anyway; mark
  it fully heart for now.
- **friend** — heart part: the whole word is irregular (the `ie` says a short-e
  sound, very unusual); mark it fully heart. Point out this is a word worth extra
  practice simply because it is used so often.
- **because** — heart part: the whole word is irregular (`au` says a short sound,
  final `se` says /z/); mark it fully heart. This is the last new heart word in
  Level 2 — all 29 are now taught (review the full list together once, aloud).

## 7. Read it (8–10 min) — Fluency routine (model→echo→choral→independent). This is
the longest text in the level, combining every taught pattern.

**Track A (child)**
> Min and Dad go to visit a friend. / They pack a backpack with a napkin / and a
> muffin. / "I want to bring the pink rabbit," / Min says, / "because it is fun." /
> Min and the friend run and jump in the grass. / When they get to the pond, /
> they sit still. / A fish splashes, / and Min spots an insect on a twig. / The
> friend is helping Min hunt for bugs. / "Should we catch it?" / the friend asks. /
> "No, / let's just watch it," / Min says, / "because it is not bad." / They pack
> up at sunset, / and hug the friend. / Min and Dad go back, / glad and full, /
> and plan to come again.

*(101 words.)*

Questions: (1) Why does Min bring the pink rabbit? (2) What do Min and the friend
decide about the insect? (3) Why do you think Min says "because it is not bad"
instead of catching it?

**Track B (adult)**
> Sam has a shift with a friend from a shop, / helping fix a big backlog of dents
> and checks. / "This job is a lot, / but his friend can help," / Sam says, /
> "because two friends finish fast." / They pack the kit, / check the list, / and
> split the job up. / Sam's friend fixes the dents, / and Sam checks the vans /
> and prints the list. / At dusk, / the shop has a full stock of fixed vans / and
> a strong stack of finished checks. / "We should do this again," / Sam's friend
> says. / "Yes, / because it went fast and we still got it done," / Sam says, /
> and they clap hands, / glad and calm.

*(97 words.)*

Questions: (1) Why does Sam say two friends finish fast? (2) What jobs did Sam and
his friend split between them? (3) Do you think Sam and his friend will work
together again? Why or why not?

## 8. Listen & Talk (5 min)
Read aloud only — no new Tier-2 word today; instead, retrieve two from across the
level and use them together:

> Retrieval passage: A stubborn old goat named Bella refused to leave the muddy
> field even when it started to rain hard. Her owner, cautious as always, did not
> want to drag a furious, wet goat across a slippery yard, so he waited, patient,
> under the barn roof. After an hour of stubborn standing in the rain, Bella
> finally got cold enough to trot inside on her own — no longer stubborn, just
> cautious about getting even wetter. Her owner laughed, dried her off, and said
> a stubborn goat always changes its mind eventually, as long as you stay cautious
> and let it happen in its own time.

Discussion questions (retrieval, both words): (1) Which word from this passage means
"not willing to change your mind"? (2) Which word means "careful, checking before
acting"? Ask the learner to also recall and use ONE more Tier-2 word from earlier in
the level (enormous, curious, furious, puzzled, exhausted, grateful, reluctant,
delighted, anxious, generous) in a sentence of their own.

## 9. Check (2 min) — this is a rehearsal for the full mastery check, not the gate
itself
5 real words spanning different families: `wish, strap, desk, wishes, jumped`
5 pseudowords: `strishing, flonked, chuffer, vandest, thramping`
1 dictated word: `napkin`
**≥90%?** → proceed to `course/level-2/mastery-check.md` (the formal exit gate).
**<90%?** → identify which specific family the error came from using the lesson list
in `README.md`'s sequence table, go back to that exact lesson's Check step reteach
instruction, and only return to the mastery check once that lesson is solid again.

## Tutor notes
- **Common error:** a learner does fine on isolated drill (step 4) but stumbles on
  the long combined text (step 7) — this is normal; the fix is more assisted reading
  practice (echo/choral) of longer combined text, not more isolated phonics drill.
  If it persists past this lesson, flag it for extra fluency-routine sessions before
  the mastery check, not as a phonics reteach.
- **Common error:** rushing through this lesson because "it's just review." Correct
  this directly with the learner: this is the single best predictor of how the
  mastery check will go, and deserves full attention.
- **For adults:** reviewing all 29 heart words aloud together (step 6) is a good
  moment to let the learner set their own pace and self-flag which 2–3 still feel
  shaky — target those specifically before the mastery check.
